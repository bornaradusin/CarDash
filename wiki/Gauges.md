Every reading at once, and the engine's fault codes. Swipe left from Home.

## The status line

| It says | What it means |
|---|---|
| OBD connected · *n* readings a second | All good |
| Dongle connected, the car isn't answering | The ignition is off |
| Connecting to the dongle… | Starting up |
| Allow CarDash to use the USB dongle | Android hasn't allowed it yet: see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting) |
| No OBD dongle found on USB | Nothing plugged in, or a USB chip CarDash doesn't know |
| Dongle problem: … Trying again… | Something went wrong; it tries again by itself |

## The tiles

Coolant, oil, boost, intake air, battery, accelerator pedal, engine load, fuel use now and air flow. Once picked in [Settings › Radio's CAN box](Radios-CAN-box), the odometer, range, fuel level and average fuel use from the CAN box are added. A reading the car doesn't report shows "–".

## Fault codes

CarDash reads the engine's fault codes when it first connects. **Scan again** reads them again. Underneath, it says whether the engine warning light is on.
